Refund Policy

Sharedock · last updated 30 August 2026

Draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyer. UK and EU consumers have statutory cancellation rights for digital purchases that interact with the 14 day period below. Have this checked before selling.

14 days, no reason needed

  • Trigger: you ask within 14 days of buying.
  • What we do: we refund your purchase in full.
  • What happens then: your licence stops. Your hosts stop serving your other devices within about a minute. Your files stay on your own hardware, unencrypted by nothing we control, and you may still reach them locally.

After 14 days

  • Trigger: Sharedock does not do something this site says it does, and we cannot fix it in a reasonable time.
  • What we do: we refund you in full, whenever you bought.

Outside that, we do not refund after 14 days. The licence is perpetual, so there is nothing left to cancel.

How to ask

Email support@REPLACE-WITH-YOUR-DOMAIN from the address on your account. You do not have to give a reason within the 14 days. We must respond within 5 working days and refund within 14 days of agreeing.

What a refund does not do

  • It does not delete your account. Use account deletion in Settings for that.
  • It does not delete your files. They are on your hardware and we cannot reach them.
