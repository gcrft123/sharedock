Privacy Policy

Sharedock · last updated 30 August 2026

Draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyer. It describes accurately what the software stores and sends. It must be checked by a qualified adviser before Sharedock is sold, particularly for UK GDPR, EU GDPR, and CCPA wording.

The short version

Your files are on your own hardware, encrypted before they leave your device. We cannot read them. What we store is the small amount needed to run accounts and connect your devices.

What we store

DataWhy
Email addressTo identify your account and send your receipt and licence.
A hash of your login keyTo check your password without ever receiving it.
Your account key, encryptedSo you can sign in on another device. We cannot decrypt it.
Device public keys and namesSo your hosts know which devices to serve.
Licence tier, seats, purchase referenceTo prove what you bought.
Share link metadataTo serve links you create. The key stays in the link, never with us.
A log of emails we sent youTo avoid sending duplicates, and to answer "did it send".

What we never store

  • Your files, or any part of them.
  • Your file names, folder names, or sizes.
  • Your master password, your recovery key, or your account key in readable form.
  • Advertising or tracking identifiers. There are none in the product.

Who else sees anything

  • Cloudflare hosts the coordination service and stores the table above. Encrypted traffic passes through it when a direct connection is not possible.
  • Stripe processes payments. Stripe receives your payment details; we never do. We receive only a payment reference.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell or share your data.

How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Purchase records: seven years after purchase, because tax law requires it.
  • Email send log: 12 months.

Your rights

  • Get a copy. Settings has an export that returns everything above as a file. It is immediate and needs no request to us.
  • Delete it. Settings has an account deletion that erases the table above. It is immediate and irreversible. Your files stay on your own hardware.
  • Correct it. You can change your email address in Settings.
  • Complain. If you are in the UK or EU you may complain to your data protection authority.

Transfers

Cloudflare processes data at locations worldwide. The stored data is limited to the table above, and file contents are never part of it.
